# Scope

## Overview

This document defines the scope of the Multi-Tenant SaaS Project Management Platform. Its purpose is to establish clear boundaries for the product, identify the business capabilities that are included, distinguish the Minimum Viable Product (MVP) from future enhancements, and provide a common understanding of what this project intends to deliver.

The scope is intended to support long-term product planning while preventing unnecessary scope expansion during development.

---

## Project Scope

The project focuses on delivering a centralized SaaS platform that enables multiple organizations to manage collaborative work within isolated environments.

The platform will provide business capabilities for:

- Managing organizations and workspaces.
- Managing members and access to organizational resources.
- Planning and tracking projects.
- Organizing work into sprints and tasks.
- Supporting collaboration through comments and file attachments.
- Providing notifications for important activities.
- Recording audit events for accountability.
- Searching across supported business resources.

The project aims to deliver these capabilities as a cohesive product rather than as independent applications.

---

## Product Scope

The product covers the complete lifecycle of collaborative project management within an organization, including:

- Organization onboarding.
- Workspace organization.
- Team collaboration.
- Project planning.
- Sprint management.
- Task execution.
- Activity tracking.
- Document collaboration.
- Operational transparency.
- Search and discovery.

The product is designed to support multiple independent organizations operating within a shared platform while maintaining logical separation between tenants.

- Human resource management.
- Customer relationship management.
- Enterprise resource planning.
- Source code hosting.
- Continuous integration and deployment services.
- Team messaging platforms.
- Video conferencing.

---

## Assumptions

The scope is based on the following assumptions:

- Organizations require isolated workspaces within a shared platform.
- Users collaborate primarily through projects and tasks.
- Organizations require role-based access to business resources.
- Project management is the primary business domain.
- Future capabilities can be introduced without redefining the core product scope.
- Product development will proceed incrementally through planned releases.

---

## Dependencies

Successful delivery of the defined scope depends on:

- Clear product requirements.
- Stable business terminology.
- Consistent product documentation.
- Defined user roles and responsibilities.
- Incremental release planning.
- Ongoing validation of business priorities.

These dependencies support effective scope management throughout the project's lifecycle.

---

## Success Criteria for Scope Completion

The project scope is considered complete when:

- All core business modules are available.
- Users can complete the primary project management workflow within the platform.
- Organizations can manage their members, workspaces, and projects independently.
- Collaboration features support everyday team activities.
- Auditability and search capabilities are available across supported business resources.
- The MVP provides a cohesive and usable product experience.
- The delivered functionality aligns with the defined project scope without introducing unnecessary business domains.

---

## Conclusion

The scope defined in this document establishes a clear and manageable boundary for the Multi-Tenant SaaS Project Management Platform. It identifies the core business capabilities required to deliver a complete project management solution while distinguishing the initial product scope from future expansion opportunities.

By maintaining a well-defined scope, the project can evolve through incremental releases without losing focus on its primary objective: delivering a production-grade SaaS platform for collaborative project management.
