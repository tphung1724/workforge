# Business Problem

## Overview

Modern organizations increasingly rely on digital collaboration to deliver projects efficiently across distributed teams, departments, and business units. As organizations grow, managing projects becomes significantly more complex due to increasing numbers of stakeholders, concurrent initiatives, and cross-functional collaboration.

Many organizations continue to rely on disconnected tools for task management, communication, document sharing, and project tracking. While these tools solve individual problems, they often fail to provide a unified view of project execution, organizational collaboration, and operational accountability.

The objective of this platform is to address these business challenges by providing a centralized Multi-Tenant SaaS Project Management Platform that enables organizations to manage projects, teams, and workspaces in a secure, scalable, and collaborative environment.

---

## Background

Organizations of different sizes—from startups to large enterprises—must coordinate multiple projects while balancing limited resources, changing priorities, and growing operational complexity.

As businesses expand, multiple departments and teams require independent workspaces while still operating under organizational governance. External collaborators, remote employees, and distributed development teams further increase the need for structured collaboration.

A centralized SaaS platform enables organizations to standardize project management practices while allowing each organization to operate independently within its own secure environment.

---

## Business Problems

The primary business problems include:

### Fragmented Collaboration

Teams frequently use separate applications for project planning, communication, documentation, and file sharing, creating fragmented workflows and duplicated information.

### Limited Organizational Visibility

Management lacks a consolidated view of project health, resource utilization, delivery progress, and team performance across workspaces.

### Inefficient Project Coordination

Project managers spend considerable effort coordinating tasks, tracking dependencies, following up on updates, and consolidating information from multiple sources.

### Inconsistent Team Processes

Different teams adopt different working practices, making governance, reporting, onboarding, and collaboration increasingly difficult.

### Growing Administrative Complexity

As organizations expand, managing members, permissions, workspaces, and project ownership becomes increasingly time-consuming.

### Insufficient Accountability

Without centralized activity tracking, organizations struggle to understand who performed specific actions, when changes occurred, and how project decisions evolved over time.

### Challenges in Multi-Organization Management

Organizations require logical isolation of their operational data while sharing a common software platform. Without proper tenant separation, operational management becomes difficult and business trust may be reduced.
